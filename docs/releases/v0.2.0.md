# v0.2.0: Campaign advisor and model evaluation

This milestone adds the Streamlit campaign advisor, frozen U.S. model integration, experimental international/mixed historical estimates, and a separate accuracy-improvement study. It does not activate the newer research candidates or establish present-day predictive accuracy.

## Application

- Streamlit entrypoint: `app.py`; Python 3.12 and pinned root requirements.
- Dark theme, readable typography, campaign validation and model-specific inputs.
- Cached loading with artifact SHA-256, metadata, runtime, class-order and schema checks. Missing/incompatible artifacts produce an error without training or a fabricated prediction.
- U.S. predictions retain HistGradientBoosting v1, its five inputs and threshold 0.5.
- International and mixed modes use separate experimental historical Logistic Regression pipelines. They use duration, category, subcategory and listed region; funding amounts are excluded because historical currency units remain unverified.
- Setup, deployment settings, model requirements and contributor credit are documented in the README and handoff.

## Active model results

| Mode | Historical development accuracy | ROC-AUC | Failure recall |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| U.S. HGB v1 | 66.93% | 0.7239 | 55.72% |
| International historical | 60.41% | 0.5893 | 30.52% |
| Mixed historical | 62.23% | 0.6631 | 50.41% |

These use different populations, inputs and validation procedures; they are not a head-to-head comparison or independent test results. International accuracy is below its 60.97% majority baseline, so it remains demonstration-only. Probabilities are uncalibrated, source data are from 2009–2012, and listed locations are not registration markets. Current campaign plans may be entered, but current accuracy has not been established.

## Separate improvement study

- Reviewed current Team 1A/1B source and explained why update-based headline scores are not pre-launch comparisons.
- Audited the existing U.S. city-frequency candidate: recorded development accuracy 67.70%; requires full city/state input. It remains unselected.
- Compared HGB, XGBoost, CatBoost and LightGBM on common international/mixed training inputs and folds, with a Logistic reference, inner family selection, fixed threshold and accuracy/AUC/failure-recall guardrails.
- Mixed LightGBM scored 63.02% accuracy; mixed HGB scored 62.75%. Both have improved failure recall and are prepared as separate research candidates. The active mixed model remains the 62.23% Logistic baseline.
- No international candidate passed all guardrails. Higher accuracy alone was insufficient where failure recall or AUC deteriorated.
- Reviewed funding/reward/title provenance and a bounded November 2026 external-data schema. No modern accuracy score or reserved-set evaluation was computed.

Full methodology, fold results, limitations and the team handoff are in `docs/scope_improvement_study_2026-11-17.md` and its linked evidence files. These remain development findings, not independent proof of candidate superiority.

## Verification and boundaries

All 96 repository tests passed in the documented combined model/dashboard test environment, including the optional boosting packages. Critical lint and whitespace checks passed. Production source/model/metadata hashes were unchanged by the separate study. The experiment did not tune on reserved labels, alter official splits, or modify the app's selected models.

No additional binary assets are uploaded with this release. GitHub source archives include the model files already tracked in the repository; ignored research candidates and private datasets are not included. Only load trusted model files with matching metadata and pinned integrity checks.
