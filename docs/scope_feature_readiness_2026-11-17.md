# Scope and feature readiness for the separate experiment

Reviewed November 17, 2026. This is a source-readiness check for the newly authorized offline experiment. It does not promote a model, change the frozen U.S. v1 contract, or authorize reserved-label evaluation. No training, model/code edits, or reserved-label reads were performed for this note. The prior [funding-currency review](funding_currency_review.md) is preserved.

The bounded follow-up found direct platform evidence that project titles, categories, and the available reward ladder can change while a campaign is live. It did not find launch snapshots or a source dictionary tying the repository's scraped fields to their launch-time values. The experiment may measure these feature groups separately, but an accuracy gain cannot supply that missing provenance.

## What original and archived sources can resolve

The original Kaggle metadata retrieved in the first review identifies the dataset/version/upload date but supplies no source dictionary or snapshot/FX method. AppsBlogger's first-person corpus account and the author-hosted Mitra/Gilbert paper support the June 2012 URL-corpus history; neither establishes that the local CSV is their exact snapshot or records launch-time title/reward values. Repeating generic Kickstarter dataset searches would not close that gap.

A useful provenance packet would contain:

1. The original scraper source/version, raw-file checksum, collection timestamp/time zone, project-ID mapping and field selectors. In particular, identify what `funded date` meant and whether `duration` was planned, elapsed, or computed.
2. Original campaign metadata or timestamped archived pages with explicit funding currency, goal, `launched_at`, deadline, and title/reward versions. A late archived capture alone cannot establish the launch reward ladder or original title. An unavailable archive is missing evidence, not evidence that no edit occurred.
3. A training-ID-only verification join first. Record coverage and discrepancies, avoid silently dropping unavailable rows, and freeze transformation decisions before any approved reserved evaluation. An archived modern page must not be substituted for the older prediction-time value.
4. If historical launch fields cannot be recovered reliably, collect a new consented/prospective launch snapshot dataset with the required units and timestamps. Use it to define a future model version rather than repair old labels by assumption.

No archived campaign pages were acquired or matched in this bounded follow-up. The official sources establish what can vary; they do not fill missing row histories. No claim that an archive contains recoverable launch snapshots is made.

## Evaluation boundary

Run the newly authorized comparisons in a separate experiment with unchanged official training membership and the frozen v1 as reference. Keep fitted preprocessing, goal references, encoding, feature selection and model/threshold choice inside each fitting fold. Report feature groups with unresolved snapshot or unit semantics as exploratory even when fold-safe. Random training folds do not demonstrate prospective performance, and provisional reconstructed dates do not establish a validated time split. Preserve existing reserved-evaluation approval requirements and do not tune on reserved scores.

## Primary sources

All official help pages below were retrieved November 17, 2026 and state August 6, 2026 as their article date. These are current platform policies, not proof that all rules were identical in 2009–2012.

- **S1:** Kickstarter, [Can a project be edited after launching?](https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236599-can-a-project-be-edited-after-launching). Lists live edits to title/category and rewards, normal funding-goal/deadline restrictions, and the support-extension exception introduced in March 2020.
- **S2:** Kickstarter, [Can I edit my rewards after I've launched my project?](https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236617-can-i-edit-my-rewards-after-i-ve-launched-my-project). Unbacked rewards can be changed/deleted and new tiers can be added while live; backed tiers have restrictions. This directly prevents assuming a later whole ladder equals the launch ladder.
- **S3:** Kickstarter, [How to set the right funding goal](https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236709-how-to-set-the-right-funding-goal-for-your-kickstarter-campaign). Defines goal-setting before launch; supplies no retrospective CSV currency conversion rule.
- **S4:** Kickstarter, [What is the maximum project duration?](https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236742-what-is-the-maximum-project-duration). Describes planned day count or deadline/time. Do not impose today's 60-day limit on the historical fractional-duration data.
- **S5:** Kickstarter, [Can I choose my project's display currency?](https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236796-can-i-choose-my-project-s-display-currency). Distinguishes project/native funding currency from a backer's preferred display currency, with explicit contemporary exceptions. Does not document the old scraper.
- **S6:** Kickstarter, [Setting up your project's pre-launch page](https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236379-setting-up-your-project-s-pre-launch-page). Establishes contemporary pre-launch title/category/location visibility, not that our historical scrape occurred before launch.
